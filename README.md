# 🧠 AI & Deafness: Exploring Fairness in Algorithms

Welcome to the **AI & Deafness Educational Project**!  
This beginner-friendly learning tool helps students explore how Artificial Intelligence (AI) impacts the Deaf and disabled community — especially in systems like **COMPAS** used in the justice system.

---

## 👀 What’s Inside
- 🧾 **Real case studies** of AI bias (COMPAS, Amazon hiring, Deaf court cases, facial recognition)
- ⚖️ **Ethics focus**: fairness, accessibility, and inclusion
- 🧪 **Mock AI Risk Calculator**: shows how small inputs change AI outcomes
- 🎓 **Student-friendly flow** with clear examples and critical thinking prompts
